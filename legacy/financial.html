Financing routes

Three ways Amara buyers typically fund a purchase.

01

Local bank mortgage

Financing through a bank in the property's own country — usually the lowest rate, but requires local income proof or a larger deposit for non-residents.

02

International / offshore lending

Specialist lenders who finance African property against income earned elsewhere — faster approval, typically a higher rate.

03

Developer payment plans

On new-build listings, staged payments tied to construction milestones — no traditional mortgage required.

Currency & transfer

All Amara listings are quoted in USD.

Most cross-border buyers settle in US dollars to keep pricing comparable market to market, then convert locally at completion. Your advisor will introduce a currency partner to lock in a transfer rate ahead of closing, so a swing in the exchange rate doesn't change your final number.

Legal and transfer fees typically run 3–7% of the purchase price depending on country — your advisor provides an exact figure before you make an offer.
